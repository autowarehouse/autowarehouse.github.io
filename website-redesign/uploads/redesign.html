01 / build route

A production line for data warehouse work.

Research pattern: data engineering buyers respond to operational clarity, asset lineage, governance, and proof. This design turns those into a visual route, not another SaaS hero.
Station 01

Survey the raw estate

Connect databases, files, documents, and APIs. Profile schema, risks, and relationships.

Station 02

Choose the model mold

Start with HRDM or define a domain model. The model becomes the contract.

Station 03

Match and inspect

AI proposes mappings; analysts review low-confidence joins and business rules.

Station 04

Run the DAG line

Generate DDL, extract data, load SCD2 dimensions, and enforce quality gates.

Station 05

Ship governed answers

Business users ask questions through TalkToHR; SQL and charts trace back to the model.

AutoWarehouse should not look like another AI dashboard.

The category talks about context layers, trust layers, automated data movement, and warehouse automation. AutoWarehouse can own a different image: the place where a messy enterprise data estate is fabricated into a governed warehouse.

BOM
Bill of model materials Templates, layers, tables, keys, SCD2 policy, semantic views.
QC
Quality control gates Every AI suggestion carries score, conflict state, and approval status.
DAG
Production routing Long-running ETL work is a traceable route, not a black-box job.
03 / audience lanes

One story, four buyer translations.

The foundry metaphor stays consistent, but each persona sees a different control point: model, governance, delivery speed, or answers.
Data teams

Less glue, more governed build work.

Profiling, model templates, mapping review, and DAG execution live in one lifecycle.

IT / Admin

Automation with inspection points.

OIDC, RBAC, audit, tenant isolation, and human approval keep AI bounded.

HR / Domain

HRDM gives the first working mold.

Employee, department, headcount, turnover, compensation, and KPI semantics are pre-shaped.

Executives

Answers trace back to the model.

TalkToHR is credible because the warehouse underneath has a governed contract.
