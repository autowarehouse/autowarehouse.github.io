01 / ENEMY MODEL

The old site sells magic. This one sells control.

The visual language should stop behaving like a generic AI SaaS page. The product is about data structure, approval gates, and repeatable execution.
01

No emoji pipeline

Use schema nodes, job states, table names, and mapping confidence instead of decorative icons.

02

No vague AI glow

AI appears as traceable suggestions, conflicts, gates, and generated artifacts.

03

No soft brochure cards

Panels feel like operational surfaces: dense, labeled, inspectable, and a little intimidating.

04

No empty promise

Every claim attaches to something concrete: HRDM, DAG worker, SCD2, NL-to-SQL, RBAC.
